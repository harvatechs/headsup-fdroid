Heads Up is a modern, privacy-respecting posture awareness and geospatial logging application.

Features:
• Live Sensor HUD: Real-time device pitch, roll, and tilt detection.
• Posture Guidance: Immediate visual feedback and optional haptic alerts when tilting downward excessively.
• Geospatial Activity: Map your posture checks and tap events on OpenStreetMap.
• Geohash Resolution: High-precision geospatial hashing for clustered location exploration.
• Analytics & Insights: Hourly distributions, posture percentage breakdowns, and activity trends.
• Offline-First & Private: Operates completely on-device without requiring third-party accounts or data harvesting.

Heads Up is 100% Free and Open Source Software.
